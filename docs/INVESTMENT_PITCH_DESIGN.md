# LooPyck Design Intelligence: The Psychology of Conversion

**Version:** 1.0.0

## 1. Executive Summary
LooPyck's design is not merely aesthetic; it is a **conversion engine** built on cognitive psychology principles. We optimize for trust, clarity, and rapid decision-making.

## 2. Cognitive UI System
### 2.1 Information Density Regulation
We employ a variable density system (`themeVariants.ts`) that adapts to the user's intent:
- **Discovery Mode (Minimal)**: Low density, large visuals. Encourages browsing.
- **Comparison Mode (Informative)**: High density, data-rich. Facilitates analysis.

### 2.2 The "Trust" Gradient
Our `AestheticEngine` uses specific color spectrums (Emerald/Blue) associated with reliability to highlight AI recommendations. Red/Amber is reserved strictly for alerts, preventing "alarm fatigue."

## 3. Social Proof Engineering
The **Viral Card V2** leverages the "Bandwagon Effect" and "Authority Bias":
- **AI Trust Score**: A quantifiable metric that simplifies the decision process ("92/100 implies safety").
- **Signature Label**: "Verified by LooPyck" acts as a stamp of authority.

## 4. Performance & ROI
By generating share assets client-side (Zero-Cost Infrastructure), we achieve:
